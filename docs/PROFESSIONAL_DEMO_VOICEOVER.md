## Plain-text read-through (copy/paste for talent)

> TipGuard SA — digital tipping for car guards and venues, built for South Africa on our own domain, tipguardsa.co.za.
>
> A customer scans a QR code and lands on our tip page — not a third-party marketplace. They sign in, choose an amount in rands, and pay through Paystack Inline, still initiated from TipGuard. Card details are entered in Paystack’s secure modal; we never store card numbers. Payment completes back on tipguardsa.co.za.
>
> On Android, an NFC tag opens the same verified tip link over HTTPS. We validate the host and path before checkout. iPhone doesn’t support Web NFC in the browser — so we guide customers to QR tipping on the identical page and the same Paystack flow. One product, two entry points.
>
> Merchants sign in to a live dashboard on tipguardsa.co.za. Tips from QR and NFC appear in the ledger — amount, time, and status — so venues reconcile payouts with confidence.
>
> Legal, privacy, refund, and contact pages live on our domain for POPIA and partner review. Settlement runs through Paystack webhooks into our backend with verified signatures and idempotent wallet credits. Today we’re in test mode; live keys follow approval.
>
> TipGuard SA — transparent tipping, secure payments, merchant-ready reporting. Thank you.

## Pronunciation & style

- **TipGuard** — two words, equal stress  
- **Paystack** — PAY-stack  
- **ZAR** — say “rands” on camera; “ZAR” OK in investor deck VO  
- Avoid: “revolutionary”, “blockchain”, “guaranteed returns”  
- Prefer: “verified”, “on our domain”, “test mode”, “merchant-ready”

---

## Technical recording

- Export WAV 48 kHz mono; sync to picture in editor  
- Room tone 2s head/tail for crossfades  
- If replacing temp guide track, mute OBS system audio during screen capture
